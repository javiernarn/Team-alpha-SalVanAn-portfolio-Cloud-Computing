SalValAn Portfolio 6.3.3  (Enter screen so the music starts by itself)
- Browsers do not allow any website to play sound before the visitor does something. The only reliable way to have the music
  start without pressing the music button is an Enter screen: the page now opens with "Enter" (logo, "Best experienced with
  sound"). The tap on Enter is the permission, the music fades in at that moment and the intro animation starts right after.
- "Enter without sound" skips the music and remembers it (also remembered when the header button is used to pause).
  A visitor who paused is not shown the Enter screen next time.
- If the browser already allows sound (for example a returning visitor on Chrome), the Enter screen is skipped and the
  music just starts. The track downloads while the Enter screen is showing.
- Settings > SalValAn > Background music = off removes the music, the button and the Enter screen.
