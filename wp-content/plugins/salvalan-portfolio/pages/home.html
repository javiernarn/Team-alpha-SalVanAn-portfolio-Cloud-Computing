How a project moves through us

Our tagline lists the roles. In practice the work runs in this order.

  1. Analysis

    Mariel gathers requirements from owners and staff, studies how the business runs today and defines what the system must do.

  2. Development

    Jonee designs and builds the system with Laravel, MySQL and a responsive frontend, then tests and deploys it.

  3. Documentation

    Diane writes the research paper, requirements and system documentation, and keeps every term consistent.
