Web-Based and Mobile Management System with One-Time Password (OTP) Authentication for A & K Car Tint and Accessories Service

A centralized digital platform that improves and simplifies the daily operations of the business. It replaces manual handling of customer information, service appointments, vehicle records, payments, inventory and staff activities. The system aims to minimize scheduling conflicts, improve employee task monitoring, manage business transactions and give customers a more convenient way to book services.

Web-based system · owner and staff

  • Customer records and staff management
  • Service management and job orders
  • Appointment booking and scheduling
  • Payment processing and staff commissions
  • Inventory monitoring
  • Service progress tracking and business transactions
  • Report generation for better decision-making

Mobile app · customers

  • Create an account and log in with OTP authentication
  • View the available services
  • Schedule appointments
  • Manage their profile
  • Monitor their booking history

Security: One-Time Password (OTP)

Users verify their identity with a one-time password sent to their registered email address, an additional layer of account security.
