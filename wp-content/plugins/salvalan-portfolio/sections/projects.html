OCCDL, OCC Duty Log

Mobile app for OJT trainees that works as a digital Daily Time Record. Hours are logged per host client, counted in real time toward the required total, and exported as a signable PDF report.

Users

  • OJT trainees, sign up with a Student ID, set required hours, log daily duty and track progress
  • OJT coordinators, receive the signable Duty Time Record report

SCLF, Smart Campus Lost & Found

Campus system for reporting lost and found items, with automatic matching, claims, incident reports, service requests and QR visitor badges.

Users

  • Students and instructors, report lost items, check matches, file claims, incidents and service requests
  • Security officers, scan QR codes, log found items, review matches, manage inventory and the claim counter
  • Administrators, manage users, review requests and follow the audit log
  • Visitors, open their QR badge on a phone, no login needed
Open live system
